Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 29, 2025

This privacy policy describes how we handle your information when you use the AssessMe Word Add-in.

Information We Collect

AssessMe stores your writing activity (including document changes, diffs, and metadata such as timestamps and document ID) securely inside the Word document itself. No personal data is automatically transmitted externally.

How We Use Your Data

AssessMe enables authorized reviewers or instructors to extract and analyze the recorded writing activity directly from the Word document. This helps assess writing behavior and progress. Data remains fully encrypted within the document unless manually shared by the user.

Your Rights and Responsibilities

As the document owner or user, you have control over the stored activity data. You are responsible for safeguarding the document. AssessMe does not transmit any recorded data to external servers automatically. However, if you share the document with external parties (such as instructors, evaluators, or others), the embedded writing data may become accessible to them.

By using this add-in, you agree not to distribute your document without understanding that all logged data travels along with the document itself.
